FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Got Questions? We Have Answers

What services does DreamBrixs offer?

We offer individual house construction, villa construction, apartment construction, residential project development, architectural & structural design, and turnkey interior solutions.

Do you provide turnkey solutions?

Yes, we provide complete end-to-end turnkey services from design and approvals to construction and final handover.

Which areas in Chennai do you serve?

We primarily work across prime locations including Anna Nagar, Boat Club, Kotturpuram, Besant Nagar, ECR, OMR, and surrounding areas.

Do you handle municipal approvals?

Yes, we manage CMDA/DTCP plan sanctions, structural drawings, and all required municipal approvals as part of our service.

What is your typical project timeline?

Timelines vary based on project size and complexity. We provide a clear schedule after site assessment and aim for on-time delivery.

Do you offer Vastu-compliant designs?

Yes, 100% Vastu compliance is integrated into our planning for individual houses and villas.

What warranty do you provide?

We offer a 10-year structural warranty along with crystalline waterproofing protection on critical areas.

How do you ensure quality?

Through NABL lab testing, certified materials, weekly site logs, full-time engineers, and strict quality control protocols.

We build with expertise and innovation, creating residential spaces that reflect quality, reliability and lasting architectural value across Chennai, Tamil Nadu.
