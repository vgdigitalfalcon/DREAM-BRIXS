QUALITY CONTROL FRAMEWORK

Engineering Integrity
Without Compromise

Chennai's tropical coastal climate demands rigorous structural specifications. We implement multi-barrier waterproofing, anti-carbonation coatings, and cathodic rebar protection across all sites.

01
CERTIFIED GRADE 550D TMT

Strict T-bar & 28-day concrete cube compressive tests logged at certified NABL laboratories before every pour.

02
INDEPENDENT NABL CUBE TESTING

Third-party verification reports issued before de-shuttering or subsequent slab pours for complete transparency.

03
10-YEAR WATER INGRESS WARRANTY

Dual-coat crystalline waterproofing membranes and polymer screeds applied on basements, bathrooms, and roof terraces.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Got Questions? We Have Answers

What is DreamBrixs' construction process?

We follow a clear 4-stage process: Understand, Plan, Execute, and Handover, ensuring complete transparency and quality at every step.

Do you offer Vastu-compliant designs?

Yes, 100% Vastu compliance is integrated into our planning for individual houses and villas across Chennai.

What warranty do you provide?

We offer a 10-year structural warranty along with crystalline waterproofing protection on critical areas.

How do you ensure zero cost overruns?

Through detailed BOQ, milestone-based billing, and weekly transparent reporting with no surprise escalations.

Which areas in Chennai do you serve?

We primarily work in Anna Nagar, Boat Club, Kotturpuram, Besant Nagar, ECR, OMR, and other prime residential neighbourhoods.

Do you handle CMDA approvals?

Yes, we manage complete plan sanctions, structural drawings, and all municipal approvals as part of our service.

Can you build coastal villas?

Absolutely. Our villas along ECR and OMR are engineered specifically for Chennai's coastal humidity and climate.

How long does a typical project take?

Timelines vary by size, but we provide clear schedules after site assessment and always aim for on-time handover.
