OUR PROCESS

How We Design Your Home?

Phase 01
Site Study & Briefing

We analyse plot dimensions, soil type, sun path, and gather your functional requirements and Vastu preferences.

Phase 02
Conceptualization (2D & 3D)

Multiple 2D layout options followed by detailed 3D exterior elevations and material selection.

Phase 03
Structural & MEP Detailing

Engineering safe load-bearing structures and designing the concealed plumbing, electrical, and HVAC routes.

Phase 04
Sanction & Working Drawings

Finalized blueprint sets for CMDA/DTCP approvals and detailed Good-for-Construction (GFC) site drawings.
