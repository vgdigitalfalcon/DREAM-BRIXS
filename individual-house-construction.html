How long does individual house construction take?

Timelines depend on size and design complexity. We provide a clear schedule after site assessment and aim for on-time handover.

Do you handle CMDA approvals?

Yes, we manage complete plan sanctions, structural drawings, and all municipal approvals.

Is Vastu compliance included?

Yes, 100% Vastu-compliant designs are integrated into our planning process.

What warranty do you offer?

We provide a 10-year structural warranty along with crystalline waterproofing protection.

Through detailed BOQ, milestone-based billing, and weekly transparent reporting.

Yes, we specialise in optimising space utilisation while maintaining comfort and Vastu compliance.
